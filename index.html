Featured Projects

1️⃣ Ace Voyage

Ace Voyage is an action‑puzzle adventure where you step into the role of Patt, an aircraft pilot whose mission is abruptly interrupted after a forced landing. Stranded and with his plane in pieces, he must venture across a variety of distinct biomes to recover the scattered components of his aircraft, all while facing enemies that challenge him throughout the journey.

🎮 Play on Itch.io 💻 GitHub Code

2️⃣ Prehistoric Trial

Prehistoric Trial is a serious game designed to serve as a foundation for administering validated psychological tests to students. The goal is to allow players to engage with the experience naturally, keeping them immersed in the game world while reducing the awareness that they are being evaluated.

🎮 Try it out 💻 GitHub Repository

3️⃣ BraveSkull: Sealbound

BraveSkull: Sealbound is a first‑person puzzle game where you take on the role of a skeleton summoned by a necromancer. Rather than serving his master, the protagonist attempts to escape, navigating a series of rooms and obstacles scattered throughout the necromancer’s castle. To succeed, he learns and wields magical runes—powers that allow him to bend the physical laws of objects and the world itself—using them to reshape his surroundings, solve puzzles, and ultimately break free.

📅 Release date: September 2026
